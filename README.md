# Komentator

**Football Talent Platform** — career infrastructure for sports communication talent in Indonesia.

> From passionate fans → professional careers in sports media, built on merit, continuous learning, and industry connection.

---

## About the product

Indonesia has millions of football fans and sports content creators, but the path into the professional sports media industry remains narrow, informal, and network-dependent.

**Komentator** bridges that gap:

| For talent | For industry |
|------------|--------------|
| A clear, visible career pathway | Structured talent discovery |
| Transparent evaluation + portfolio | Trusted evidence of capability |
| Continuous learning & mentorship | Lower-risk recruitment |
| Connection to real opportunities | A growing talent pipeline |

Competitions are the entry point. The **platform** is the long-term product.

## Core principles

- **Merit over connections** — opportunity is earned through demonstrated ability
- **Learning over winning** — success is measured by how many people improve, not only by champions
- **Infrastructure over events** — competitions are the entry point; the platform is the product
- **Human judgment + technology** — AI accelerates evaluation; experts retain professional judgment
