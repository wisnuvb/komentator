## User Journey

Version 0.1 (Working Draft)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the end-to-end journey experienced by users throughout their relationship with Football Talent Platform.

The objective is not to describe application screens or technical workflows, but to understand how users progress from initial awareness to long-term participation and professional opportunity.

The journey described here is a behavioral model and should evolve as new user research becomes available.

---

# Journey Philosophy

Football Talent Platform is not designed around competitions.

It is designed around long-term career progression.

Competitions serve as important milestones within a much longer journey.

The product should continuously help users answer a single question:

> **"What is my next meaningful step?"**

---

# Core Journey Overview

```text
Discover

↓

Explore

↓

Join

↓

Demonstrate Ability

↓

Receive Evaluation

↓

Improve

↓

Build Reputation

↓

Access Opportunities

↓

Give Back
```

The journey is continuous rather than linear.

Users may repeat several stages as they continue developing their careers.

---

# Stage 1 — Discover

## User Mindset

"I enjoy football and I think I have potential."

Users first become aware of the platform through:

* Social media
* Football communities
* Friends
* Creator recommendations
* Competitions
* Industry promotion

---

## User Goals

* Understand what the platform is.
* Determine whether it is relevant.
* See inspiring success stories.
* Decide whether participation is worthwhile.

---

## Questions Users Ask

* Is this for someone like me?
* Do I need professional experience?
* Can someone from my city participate?
* What makes this different from YouTube or TikTok?

---

## Desired Outcome

The user believes:

"This could help me grow."

---

# Stage 2 — Explore

## User Mindset

"I want to know whether this platform fits my goals."

Users begin exploring:

* Career pathways
* Previous participants
* Evaluation standards
* Learning opportunities
* Industry partners

---

## User Goals

* Build confidence.
* Understand expectations.
* Reduce uncertainty.

---

## Desired Outcome

"I understand how this platform can help my career."

---

# Stage 3 — Join

## User Mindset

"I am ready to participate."

Joining represents an emotional commitment rather than simply creating an account.

The user decides to invest time, effort, and energy into becoming part of the ecosystem.

---

## User Goals

* Register.
* Build an initial profile.
* Submit required materials.
* Become an active participant.

---

## Desired Outcome

"I am officially part of this community."

---

# Stage 4 — Demonstrate Ability

## User Mindset

"I want people to see what I can actually do."

Participants showcase their communication skills through structured activities.

At this stage, visibility comes from demonstrated performance rather than existing popularity.

---

## User Goals

* Demonstrate capability.
* Gain recognition.
* Receive meaningful evaluation.

---

## Desired Outcome

"My work has been seen."

---

# Stage 5 — Receive Evaluation

## User Mindset

"I want to understand where I stand."

Evaluation should provide:

* Recognition of strengths.
* Identification of improvement areas.
* Credible assessment.
* Clear next steps.

Users should leave this stage with greater clarity rather than uncertainty.

---

## Desired Outcome

"I know what to improve next."

---

# Stage 6 — Improve

## User Mindset

"I want to become better."

Improvement transforms participation from an event into an ongoing journey.

Learning may occur through:

* Practice
* Mentorship
* Community
* Structured challenges
* Professional feedback

The specific implementation is intentionally out of scope for this document.

---

## Desired Outcome

"I am making measurable progress."

---

# Stage 7 — Build Reputation

## User Mindset

"My improvement should become visible."

As participants continue developing, they gradually build a professional reputation supported by observable achievements rather than personal claims.

Potential signals may include:

* Verified participation
* Performance history
* Learning progress
* Community contributions
* Professional recognition

The exact reputation model will be defined separately.

---

## Desired Outcome

"My profile represents my real capabilities."

---

# Stage 8 — Access Opportunities

## User Mindset

"I want my effort to lead somewhere meaningful."

Professional opportunities may include:

* Recruitment
* Collaboration
* Internship
* Broadcasting opportunities
* Club engagement
* Brand partnerships
* Community leadership

The platform's role is to increase visibility and trust rather than guarantee employment.

---

## Desired Outcome

"My skills create real opportunities."

---

# Stage 9 — Give Back

## User Mindset

"I want to help others grow."

Experienced participants strengthen the ecosystem by becoming:

* Mentors
* Judges
* Community leaders
* Guest speakers
* Industry contributors

A healthy ecosystem creates future opportunities through its own members.

---

## Desired Outcome

"I am contributing to the next generation."

---

# Journey Loops

The journey is intentionally cyclical.

```text
Learn
    ↓
Practice
    ↓
Evaluation
    ↓
Improvement
    ↓
Recognition
    ↓
Opportunity
    ↓
Learn Again
```

Every completed opportunity should increase future capability rather than end the relationship with the platform.

# Success Criteria

A successful journey is achieved when users:

* Understand what meaningful progress looks like.
* Continue participating beyond a single competition.
* Feel their abilities are fairly recognized.
* Perceive continuous personal growth.
* Build trusted professional credibility.
* Gain access to meaningful opportunities.
* Remain engaged with the ecosystem after achieving success.

---

# Journey Principles

Every stage should answer one essential question for the user:

* **Discover:** Why should I care?
* **Explore:** Is this for me?
* **Join:** How do I begin?
* **Demonstrate Ability:** How can I show my skills?
* **Receive Evaluation:** How am I doing?
* **Improve:** What should I do next?
* **Build Reputation:** How do others recognize my progress?
* **Access Opportunities:** Where can this take me?
* **Give Back:** How can I help others succeed?

If any stage fails to answer its core question, the overall journey becomes fragmented.

---

# Document Principle

This document intentionally focuses on user transformation rather than product functionality.

Specific features, application flows, technical implementation, and interaction design will be defined in subsequent documents.

Every future feature should strengthen at least one stage of this journey or remove friction that prevents users from progressing to the next stage.
