# Football Talent Platform

## User Personas

Version 0.1 (Working Draft)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the primary users of Football Talent Platform.

The purpose of these personas is not to describe demographic profiles, but to understand user motivations, behaviors, frustrations, decision-making processes, and success criteria.

All personas are working hypotheses that require validation through interviews and observation.

---

# Platform Perspective

Football Talent Platform is a two-sided platform.

It must simultaneously create value for:

* Talent seeking opportunities.
* Organizations seeking trusted talent.

The platform cannot succeed by serving only one side.

---

# Persona T-01 — Emerging Football Creator

## Overview

An independent football content creator who is passionate about football and dreams of entering the professional sports media industry.

They already create content consistently but are uncertain how to progress beyond social media.

---

## Typical Profile

* Age: Approximately 18–28
* Lives in any region of Indonesia
* Active on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or podcasts
* Produces football-related content regularly
* Has little or no professional industry experience

---

## Goals

* Become recognized for communication ability rather than popularity alone.
* Build credibility within the sports industry.
* Improve professional skills.
* Gain opportunities to work with broadcasters, clubs, or sports organizations.
* Receive meaningful feedback from experienced professionals.

---

## Current Behaviors

* Watches professional commentators to learn.
* Creates football-related content independently.
* Participates in online football discussions.
* Seeks exposure through social media.
* Occasionally joins local competitions or creator communities.

---

## Pain Points

* Unsure what skills the industry actually values.
* Limited access to experienced mentors.
* No structured roadmap for professional growth.
* Difficulty gaining visibility without a large audience.
* Receives little actionable feedback.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I know what I need to improve, people recognize my ability, and I have realistic opportunities to work professionally."

# Persona T-02 — Aspiring Sports Media Student

## Overview

A university student or recent graduate interested in sports broadcasting, journalism, or communication.

Possesses academic knowledge but limited practical exposure.

---

## Goals

* Build a professional portfolio.
* Gain real-world experience.
* Connect with practitioners.
* Increase employability after graduation.

---

## Current Behaviors

* Participates in campus organizations.
* Studies communication or journalism.
* Practices presentation or broadcasting.
* Looks for internships and competitions.

---

## Pain Points

* Limited industry access.
* Few opportunities to receive professional evaluation.
* Difficulty standing out among graduates.
* Portfolio lacks external recognition.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I graduate with practical experience, industry recognition, and clear career opportunities."

---

# Persona D-01 — Talent Acquisition / Producer

## Overview

Works for a broadcaster, sports media company, football club, or event organizer.

Responsible for identifying presenters, commentators, hosts, or other sports communication talent.

---

## Goals

* Find capable talent efficiently.
* Reduce recruitment risk.
* Identify candidates with long-term potential.
* Build a reliable pipeline of future talent.

---

## Current Behaviors

* Relies on recommendations.
* Searches social media manually.
* Reviews creator portfolios.
* Uses personal networks.
* Conducts auditions when necessary.

---

## Pain Points

* Difficult to evaluate unknown talent consistently.
* Time-consuming discovery process.
* Limited visibility into emerging creators.
* High uncertainty when hiring unfamiliar candidates.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I can confidently identify high-potential talent using trusted information rather than relying only on recommendations."

---

# Persona D-02 — Brand or Sponsor Representative

## Overview

Represents a commercial brand seeking credible football personalities for campaigns, events, or community engagement.

---

## Goals

* Find authentic football personalities.
* Collaborate with creators aligned with the brand.
* Reduce campaign risk.
* Discover emerging talent before competitors.

---

## Pain Points

* Difficult to assess professionalism.
* Follower count alone is insufficient.
* Limited access to new creators outside existing networks.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I can discover authentic football personalities who represent our brand effectively."

---

# Shared Needs Across Talent Personas

Regardless of background, most aspiring participants appear to seek:

* Recognition based on demonstrated ability.
* Clear career direction.
* Constructive professional feedback.
* Continuous learning.
* Fair opportunities regardless of location.
* Industry visibility.

These assumptions require validation.

---

# Shared Needs Across Industry Personas

Organizations consistently appear to value:

* Trust.
* Efficiency.
* Consistent evaluation.
* Lower recruitment risk.
* Access to emerging talent.
* Long-term relationships.

These assumptions also require validation.

# Validation Priorities

The following assumptions should be validated through interviews:

* Which goals are most important for creators?
* Do participants value recognition more than prize money?
* What information increases recruiter confidence?
* What barriers most often prevent creators from pursuing professional careers?
* Which signals do organizations currently use to judge talent quality?

Persona definitions should evolve continuously as new evidence is collected.

---

# Document Principle

These personas are behavioral models rather than fixed demographic categories.

They exist to guide product decisions, user experience, feature prioritization, and research activities.

Whenever evidence contradicts a persona, the persona should be updated rather than forcing the product to fit outdated assumptions.
