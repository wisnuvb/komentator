# Football Talent Platform

## Success Metrics

Version 0.1 (Working Draft)

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Football Talent Platform measures meaningful progress.

The objective is not simply to monitor business performance, but to determine whether the platform is successfully creating long-term value for talent, industry, and the broader ecosystem.

Metrics should support better product decisions rather than encourage short-term optimization.

---

# Measurement Philosophy

Football Talent Platform exists to create meaningful career opportunities.

Therefore, success cannot be measured solely by:

* Registered users
* Competition participants
* Revenue
* Website traffic

These metrics are important, but they are outcomes—not the ultimate purpose.

The platform should primarily measure whether it is improving the quality, accessibility, and sustainability of sports communication careers.

---

# North Star Metric

## Trusted Career Opportunities Created

The long-term North Star is the number of meaningful career opportunities that occur because of the platform.

Examples include:

* Recruitment
* Internships
* Freelance projects
* Broadcasting opportunities
* Club collaborations
* Event hosting
* Brand partnerships

This metric best reflects whether the platform is fulfilling its mission of connecting talent with industry.

---

# Supporting Success Dimensions

The North Star is influenced by five supporting dimensions:

1. Talent Growth
2. Industry Trust
3. Ecosystem Activity
4. Platform Sustainability
5. Learning & Improvement

Each dimension should be monitored independently.

---

# Talent Growth Metrics

These metrics evaluate whether participants continue progressing.

Potential indicators include:

* Percentage of participants who return after their first activity.
* Percentage of participants completing multiple development cycles.
* Percentage receiving professional feedback.
* Percentage showing measurable improvement over time.
* Growth of verified professional portfolios.

Desired Trend:

Participants continue developing rather than leaving after one event.

---

# Industry Trust Metrics

These metrics evaluate whether organizations trust the platform.

Potential indicators include:

* Organizations returning to recruit again.
* Number of repeat recruitment activities.
* Percentage of opportunities resulting in successful collaboration.
* Satisfaction from industry partners.
* Diversity of participating organizations.

Desired Trend:

Industry increasingly treats the platform as a trusted source of emerging talent.

---

# Ecosystem Health Metrics

These metrics evaluate whether the ecosystem becomes stronger over time.

Potential indicators include:

* Community participation.
* Mentor involvement.
* Judge participation.
* Alumni engagement.
* Knowledge sharing.
* Participant referrals.
* Cross-community collaboration.

Desired Trend:

The community becomes increasingly self-sustaining.

---

# Platform Health Metrics

These metrics measure whether users continue finding value.

Potential indicators include:

* Active participants.
* Completion rates.
* Retention across multiple events.
* Profile completeness.
* Reputation growth.
* User satisfaction.

Desired Trend:

Participants remain engaged because they perceive continuous value.

---

# Business Health Metrics

Business metrics ensure long-term sustainability without becoming the primary objective.

Potential indicators include:

* Partner retention.
* Sponsor retention.
* Revenue diversification.
* Customer acquisition efficiency.
* Operational sustainability.

Desired Trend:

Revenue grows alongside ecosystem value rather than at its expense.

---

# Learning Metrics

The platform should continuously validate product assumptions.

Examples include:

* Which feedback formats produce the greatest improvement?
* Which activities create the highest retention?
* Which opportunities generate the strongest career outcomes?
* Which participant segments receive the greatest value?
* Which evaluation methods best predict professional success?

Learning metrics support better future product decisions.

---

# Guardrail Metrics

Some metrics protect the quality of the ecosystem.

Examples include:

## Fairness

Participation opportunities remain accessible regardless of geography.

---

## Trust

Evaluation remains transparent and credible.

---

## Quality

Industry confidence does not decline as participation grows.

---

## Accessibility

Growth does not create unnecessary barriers for emerging talent.

---

## Community

Competition does not undermine collaboration.

These metrics help prevent growth from weakening the platform's core values.

# Success by Product Stage

## Stage 1 — Validation

Primary Question

Do users perceive meaningful value?

Focus:

* User interviews.
* Retention.
* Feedback quality.
* Repeat participation.

Revenue is not the primary success indicator.

---

## Stage 2 — Product-Market Fit

Primary Question

Do users voluntarily return and recommend the platform?

Focus:

* Retention.
* Referral.
* Industry participation.
* Repeat recruitment.

---

## Stage 3 — Ecosystem Growth

Primary Question

Does the ecosystem become increasingly self-sustaining?

Focus:

* Network effects.
* Repeat opportunities.
* Community leadership.
* Long-term engagement.

---

## Stage 4 — Sustainable Platform

Primary Question

Can the platform continuously create career opportunities at scale while maintaining trust?

Focus:

* Opportunity creation.
* Partner retention.
* Ecosystem balance.
* Sustainable business growth.

---

# Metrics to Avoid

The following metrics should never become primary measures of success in isolation:

* Total registrations.
* Social media followers.
* Website traffic.
* Competition attendance.
* Number of competitions.
* Total prize value.
* Revenue without user value.

These metrics provide context but do not prove that the platform is achieving its mission.

---

# Success Review Cadence

Success should be reviewed at multiple levels:

* Product experiments (continuous).
* Monthly product review.
* Quarterly strategy review.
* Annual ecosystem review.

Each review should answer:

> Are we creating more meaningful opportunities than before?

---

# Document Principle

Every metric included in this document should answer one of two questions:

1. Are participants becoming more successful?
2. Is the ecosystem becoming healthier?

If a metric cannot answer either question, it should not become a primary success metric for Football Talent Platform.
